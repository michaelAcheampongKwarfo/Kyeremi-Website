Tracking takes seconds. Planning is done for you.

Most budgeting apps make you do the work: categories, spreadsheets, limits. With Kyeremi you log what you spend, and the planning happens on its own.

What you do

Log it and move on

  • Amount, category, save: about five seconds per expense
  • Quick amounts like +₵5 and +₵20 for trotro, food and airtime
  • Long-press the Kyeremi icon to log cash without opening the app
  • An evening cash check-in, and if you like, your Safe-to-Spend first thing in the morning
  • On Android, MoMo and bank SMS can be logged for you; just confirm them
  • Move money between your own accounts without it counting as spending
  • Pay a bill with one tap when it's done
What Kyeremi does

The planning, every day

  • Holds back bills due before payday and money for your goals
  • Spreads the rest over the days until money comes in
  • Takes each expense straight off today's figure
  • Underspend and tomorrow goes up; overspend and it adjusts quietly
  • Spots your biggest change each week and suggests one next step

Honest numbers, never inflated.

Safe-to-Spend is exact arithmetic on what you tell Kyeremi. Enter real balances and log what you spend, and the number you see is the number you can actually use.

  • Money you're expecting isn't counted until it arrives, so a late salary never makes today look richer than it is.
  • Bills and savings are taken out first, so they never show up as spendable.
  • A safety buffer keeps an amount you choose permanently out of view.
  • Old savings are handled honestly. Money saved before you joined isn't counted twice.
Keep it accurate

Three habits

  1. Enter balances as they really are, checked in your MoMo menu (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AT Money) or banking app. Don't round up.
  2. Log cash the same day. The evening check-in helps.
  3. If a balance drifts, correct it in Profile. Your history stays as it is.

Private by design.

Your money records live on your phone first, with a private backup that only you can restore.

Stored on your phone

Your accounts, transactions, bills and goals are saved in Kyeremi's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it works with no internet at all.

App lock with your fingerprint or face

Turn on App lock and Kyeremi opens only with your phone's own fingerprint, face unlock or passcode. Kyeremi never stores a PIN of its own.

Backup, only what's needed

Lose your phone, not your records. Sign in on a new phone and your accounts, transactions, bills and goals come back, private to your account. Never full account numbers, never your PIN.

Android first, iPhone next

Why Android first?

Most phones in Ghana run Android, so starting there means Kyeremi reaches the most people sooner, and we can make it fast on everyday phones, not just flagships.

  • Built for real phones: works offline, light on data and storage.
  • Automatic MoMo logging on Android: with your permission, Kyeremi logs MoMo and bank transactions from their text messages (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and Fidelity Bank first, more to follow). They're read on your phone and wait for you to confirm. iPhone doesn't let apps read texts, so there you'll paste a message instead.
  • iPhone is planned. Kyeremi is built so the iPhone version can follow, with quick logging, App lock with Face ID, and Sign in with Apple. Join the waitlist to hear when it's ready.

Made for how Ghana gets paid.

Salary at month end, weekly wages or daily sales from the stall: Kyeremi plans around how you actually earn.

  • MoMo, bank and cash side by side, in cedis
  • Daily earners plan a week at a time, so a good day isn't spent in one go
  • Susu, rent and data bundles set aside before payday

Questions

Is Kyeremi a bank or a MoMo wallet?

No. Kyeremi never holds, sends or receives money. It's a planning tool: you tell it what you have, and it works out what's safe to spend. Your money stays exactly where it is.

Does Kyeremi connect to my MoMo or bank account?

No, and it will never ask for your PIN. You enter your balances yourself and log what you spend. Kyeremi will never ask for your MoMo PIN or bank password. If anyone claiming to be Kyeremi asks for one, it isn't us.

Is Kyeremi free?

Yes. Kyeremi is free to use. Later, we plan to add optional premium features for people who want extra tools. If we do, we'll tell you first, and you'll never be charged unless you choose to upgrade.

When can I use it?

Early 2027, on Android first. We're testing Kyeremi now and fixing what testers find before we open it up. Join the waitlist and you'll be among the first to get it. We'll email or text you once when it's ready, and never spam you.

On iPhone? Tap iPhone when you join and we'll tell you when that version is ready.

I have an iPhone. Is Kyeremi for me?

Yes. Kyeremi is coming to iPhone too. We're starting on Android because most phones in Ghana run it, and it lets us make Kyeremi fast on everyday phones (more on why).

The iPhone version is planned with everything that matters: Safe-to-Spend, bills, goals, insights, quick cash logging, App lock with Face ID and Sign in with Apple. The one thing iPhone won't have is automatic logging from text messages, because Apple doesn't let apps read them. You'll still be able to copy a MoMo message and paste it into Kyeremi.

Join the waitlist and tap iPhone under "Your phone". The more iPhone users sign up, the sooner it comes, and we'll let you know the moment it's ready.

I don't earn a monthly salary. Will it work for me?

Yes. Tell Kyeremi how your money comes in: daily sales, weekly pay, a monthly salary, or "it varies" for contracts, gigs and remittances. It spreads what you have over the days until your next money arrives.

Will it use a lot of data?

Very little. Kyeremi works offline, so you can check what's safe to spend and log what you spend with no connection. It backs up when you're online, and it's built to run well on everyday phones, not just expensive ones.

Will Kyeremi read my text messages?

Only if you turn it on. On Android, automatic logging is off until you switch it on in Settings and allow it. Kyeremi then reads only new messages from MoMo and bank senders (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and Fidelity Bank for now), on your phone. It never opens your inbox or old messages, every other text is ignored, and the messages themselves are never uploaded. Each one waits for you to confirm, and you can turn it off at any time (privacy policy).

You can also share or paste a single MoMo message into Kyeremi yourself.

What happens to my data?

It stays yours. Your records are kept on your phone and backed up so a new phone can restore them, readable only by you. You can delete your account and everything with it from the app at any time. We never sell your data. Read the privacy policy.
