Tracking takes seconds. Planning is done for you.

Most budgeting apps make you do the work: categories, spreadsheets, limits. With Verge you log what you spend, and the planning happens on its own.

What you do

Log it and move on

  • Amount, category, save: about five seconds per expense
  • Quick amounts like +₵5 and +₵20 for trotro, food and airtime
  • Long-press the Verge icon to log cash without opening the app
  • An 8 pm "Any cash spending today?" reminder, so nothing slips
  • Pay a bill with one tap when it's done
What Verge does

The planning, every day

  • Holds back bills due before payday and money for your goals
  • Spreads the rest over the days until money comes in
  • Takes each expense straight off today's figure
  • Underspend and tomorrow goes up; overspend and it adjusts quietly
  • Spots your biggest change each week and suggests one next step

Honest numbers, never inflated.

Safe-to-Spend is exact arithmetic on what you tell Verge. Enter real balances and log what you spend, and the number you see is the number you can actually use.

  • Money you're expecting isn't counted until it arrives, so a late salary never makes today look richer than it is.
  • Bills and savings are taken out first, so they never show up as spendable.
  • A safety buffer keeps an amount you choose permanently out of view.
  • Old savings are handled honestly. Money saved before you joined isn't counted twice.
Keep it accurate

Three habits

  1. Enter balances as they really are, checked in your MoMo menu (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AT Money) or banking app. Don't round up.
  2. Log cash the same day. The 8 pm check-in helps.
  3. If a balance drifts, correct it in Profile. Your history stays as it is.

Private by design.

Your money records live on your phone first, with a private backup that only you can restore.

Stored on your phone

Your accounts, transactions, bills and goals are saved in Verge's private storage on your phone. Other apps can't read it, and it works with no internet at all.

App lock with your fingerprint or face

Turn on App lock and Verge opens only with your phone's own fingerprint, face unlock or passcode. Verge never stores a PIN of its own.

Backup, only what's needed

Lose your phone, not your records. Sign in on a new phone and your accounts, transactions, bills and goals come back, readable only by you. Never full account numbers, never your PIN.

Questions

Is Verge a bank or a MoMo wallet?

No. Verge never holds, sends or receives money. It's a planning tool: you tell it what you have, and it works out what's safe to spend.

Does Verge connect to my MoMo or bank account?

No. You enter your balances yourself and log what you spend. Verge will never ask for your MoMo PIN or bank password.

When can I use it?

We're testing Verge now. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once when it's ready on Android.

What about iPhone?

Android comes first because most phones in Ghana run it. An iPhone version is planned; here's why and what's coming. Join the waitlist to hear when it's ready.

What happens to my data?

Your records are kept on your phone and backed up so a new phone can restore them, readable only by you. You can delete your account and everything with it from the app at any time. We never sell your data. Read the privacy policy.
