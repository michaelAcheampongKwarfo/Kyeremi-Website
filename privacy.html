Who we are

Kyeremi is a personal finance app run by Kwarfo Michael Acheampong, an individual in Ghana who is responsible for your information as the data controller ("Kyeremi", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Kyeremi app and this website collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Questions or requests: our support email.

What we collect

When you create an account

You only need an account if you turn on backup. If you keep your records on this phone only, Kyeremi has no account for you and we hold none of the information below; the first name you give in the app stays on your phone.

  • Your name (first and last).
  • Your email address, if you sign in with Google, and the basic profile Google shares with us (name and email).
  • Your phone number. Until you confirm it with a text-message code, it is stored as unverified and is not used to sign in.

What you enter in the app

  • Your accounts (for example "MTN MoMo", "Telecel Cash", "AT Money" or a bank name), their balances, and optionally the last 4 digits of a phone or account number to tell them apart. We never ask for or store full account numbers.
  • Transactions you log: amount, category, account, date and any note you add.
  • Bills, your payday schedule, savings goals and contributions, and your safety buffer.

Where your money records are kept, and why

Your accounts, balances, transactions, bills, goals and safety buffer are always kept on your phone, in Kyeremi's private app storage. When you set Kyeremi up, you choose whether they also have a backup. Nothing is picked for you, and you can change it any time in Settings.

  • On your phone: every screen reads from your phone, so Kyeremi works fully without internet or data bundles. Android keeps each app's storage separate, so other apps can't read Kyeremi's data, and phones running modern Android encrypt this storage.
  • This phone only: nothing leaves your phone and you don't need an account. If you lose your phone, reset it or delete Kyeremi, your records can't be brought back, by you or by us.
  • Back up, encrypted: a few seconds after you make a change (and whenever you're back online), Kyeremi locks it on your phone and then copies it to your backup, so a lost or new phone doesn't mean lost records.

We have turned off Android's automatic app backup for Kyeremi, so your records are not also copied into a Google Drive backup without you knowing. Kyeremi's own backup, if you turn it on, is the only copy outside your phone.

How the backup is locked

  • Locked before it leaves your phone. Every record is encrypted on your phone (AES-256-GCM) with a key that is made on your phone. Our servers receive and keep only the locked copy. We can't read it, and neither can our database provider or anyone who gets into our database.
  • What our servers can see: that you have a backup, how many records it holds and roughly how big they are, and when each one last changed. Not what they are: not names, amounts, notes, categories or even whether a record is a transaction or a bill.
  • Your key stays with you. It is kept in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore or the iPhone Keychain). Android also keeps a copy in your Google account through Google's Block Store, locked with your phone's screen lock, so neither Google nor we can read it; a new phone set up from your Google account gets it back by itself. On iPhone, the copy is kept in your iCloud Keychain.
  • Your recovery key. When you turn on backup, Kyeremi shows you a recovery key (it starts with KYRM) to save somewhere safe. It opens your backup on a phone that doesn't have the automatic copy, including when you move between Android and iPhone. We don't keep it and can't send it to you again. Our servers keep only your backup's key locked with your recovery key, which we can't open. While you still have your phone, you can make a new recovery key in Settings.
  • If you lose both your phone's copy of the key and your recovery key, your backup can't be opened, not even by us. You can then start a new backup, which deletes the old one.
  • Never backed up: full account or card numbers (Kyeremi never has them), your MoMo PIN or passwords, the text of your messages, and phone-only settings such as reminder times and App lock.
  • Where it's stored: with our database provider, Supabase, whose servers may be outside Ghana. Our database only lets each person read and change their own (locked) records.
  • How long it's kept: until you turn backup off or delete your account. Turning backup off deletes the locked copy on our servers, the locked key and the automatic copy of your key; the records on your phone stay. Records you delete in the app are marked deleted inside your locked backup too, so they disappear from your other phones.

New or lost phone? Install Kyeremi and sign in with the same account. Your backup opens by itself if the phone has the automatic copy of your key; otherwise Kyeremi asks for your recovery key. Changes made while offline are backed up when you reconnect.

App lock

You can turn on App lock in Settings → Security. Kyeremi then asks for your phone's own fingerprint, face unlock or passcode every time it opens, and again if you've been away for more than a minute. Kyeremi never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or passcode: your phone checks them and only tells Kyeremi whether the check passed.

Settings that stay on your phone

Your reminder time, App lock setting and similar preferences are kept only on your phone. Daily check-in reminders are scheduled on your phone and need no server.

On this website

If you join the waitlist, we store the email address or phone number you give us, the date, the part of the page you signed up from, which phone you use and how you heard about Kyeremi if you choose to tell us, the tag on the link you arrived from (for example a link we shared on WhatsApp), and whether you offered to test Kyeremi before launch. We use these only to decide where to tell people about Kyeremi and when to build the iPhone version. The website has no advertising or tracking cookies.

What we don't collect

  • Your MoMo PIN, bank passwords or card numbers.
  • Your location or contacts.
  • Your text messages. Optional SMS capture (below) reads money messages on your phone; the messages themselves are never sent to us.

Automatic SMS capture (Android, optional)

On Android you can let Kyeremi log your MoMo and bank transactions from their text messages. Kyeremi asks once while you set it up ("Read money texts for you?"), where you can choose Turn on or Not now, and you can change it any time in Settings → Auto-capture. It is off unless you turn it on, and Kyeremi explains it before Android asks for the "receive SMS" permission.

  • New messages only. Kyeremi receives SMS as they arrive. It does not open your inbox or read older messages.
  • Only money senders. Kyeremi reads messages only from the mobile money and bank senders it supports: MTN MoMo ("MobileMoney"), Telecel Cash ("T-CASH"), Fidelity Bank ("FIDELITY"), Zenith Bank ("ZENITHBANK"), Consolidated Bank ("CBG"), Ecobank ("ECOBANK"), CalBank ("CALBANK") and Affinity ("AFFINITY"). We update this list whenever we add a sender. Every other message is ignored as it arrives, without being read or kept.
  • Read on your phone. The amount, the other party, any fee and the date are worked out on your phone, even offline. A money message waits in Kyeremi's private storage on your phone until it has been read, then it is removed. If Kyeremi can't read one yet (a new format, or an account you haven't added), it keeps that message on your phone for up to 30 days so you can check it and log it yourself, then deletes it. The text of your messages is never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • You confirm each one. Each message becomes a transaction waiting in Needs Review. Like transactions you type in, it stays on your phone, and is backed up, locked, only if you turned backup on (see "How the backup is locked").
  • Remembered on your phone: which category you chose for a person or shop, so their next message starts there, which messages were already added, so none is logged twice, and the latest balance each money message showed, so Kyeremi can tell you when its figure for that account doesn't match. These stay on your phone.
  • New message formats. Once a day Kyeremi downloads a small file listing the money message formats it can read, so it learns new ones without an app update. Everyone gets the same file, and nothing about you or your messages is sent to download it. Kyeremi uses the file only if it carries our digital signature.
  • Turn it off any time in Settings → Auto-capture, or remove the SMS permission in your phone's settings.

You can also share or paste a single money SMS into Kyeremi yourself, on any phone. It is read the same way, on your phone.

How we use your information

  • If you turn on backup: to create and secure your account, sign you in and keep your locked backup.
  • To show your Safe-to-Spend, bills, goals and insights. These are worked out from what you enter.
  • To reply when you contact us. If you use "Report a problem", your email includes your app version and phone model so we can help.
  • To email or text you once when Kyeremi launches, if you joined the waitlist. If you also offered to test Kyeremi, we contact you a few times about testing: the invitation, and to ask what you think.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, or use it for anyone else's marketing.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run Kyeremi:

  • Supabase: our account and database provider (sign-in, your profile and your locked backup if you turn backup on, the waitlist, and the file of money message formats). It holds your backup only in its locked form.
  • Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are. On Android with backup on, Google's Block Store keeps the copy of your backup key in your Google account, locked with your screen lock so Google can't read it.
  • Apple: on iPhone with backup on, your iCloud Keychain keeps the copy of your backup key, end-to-end encrypted by Apple.
  • Resend: to send sign-in codes by email, if you choose "Continue with email". Resend receives your email address and the code.
  • Vynfy: our SMS provider in Ghana, to send sign-in codes by text message when phone sign-in is available, and the launch text if you joined the waitlist with your phone number. Vynfy receives your phone number and the message.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Ghana. We choose providers that protect data with encryption and access controls. We may also disclose information if Ghanaian law requires it.

How we protect it

  • Your money records stay on your phone, in storage other apps can't read.
  • If you turn on backup, every record is locked on your phone before it's sent, with a key we never have.
  • Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • Our database only lets each person read and change their own records.
  • App lock uses your phone's fingerprint, face unlock or passcode (see above).
  • We collect as little as possible: no full account numbers, no PINs, no location.

How long we keep it

If you have an account, we keep your account information until you delete it, and your locked backup until you turn backup off or delete your account. Records on your phone stay there until you delete them in Kyeremi (Settings → Delete everything), sign out, delete your account or uninstall the app. Waitlist emails and phone numbers are kept until Kyeremi launches and we have contacted you, or until you ask us to remove yours.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, and you can object to how we use it.

  • Delete everything: on this phone only, go to Profile → Settings → Delete everything; we hold nothing else about you. With backup on, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and data, or turn backup off to delete only the backup. Can't use the app? See Delete my account.
  • Anything else: email our support email. We reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission, the regulator for personal data in Ghana.

Children

Kyeremi is for people aged 18 and over. When you set Kyeremi up, you confirm that you are 18 or older, and the app is listed for adults in app stores. Because Kyeremi never holds money, we don't ask for ID documents to check age. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
