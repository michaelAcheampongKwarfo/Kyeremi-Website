Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

The short version. Kyeremi never holds or moves your money and never asks for your MoMo PIN or bank password. We collect only what Kyeremi needs to work, we never sell your data, and you can delete your account and everything with it at any time.

Who we are

Kyeremi is a personal finance app run by Kwarfo Michael Acheampong, an individual in Ghana who is responsible for your information as the data controller ("Kyeremi", "we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Kyeremi app and this website collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written to meet Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Questions or requests: our support email.

What we collect

When you create an account

  • Your name (first and last).
  • Your email address, if you sign in with Google, and the basic profile Google shares with us (name and email).
  • Your phone number. Until you confirm it with a text-message code, it is stored as unverified and is not used to sign in.

What you enter in the app

  • Your accounts (for example "MTN MoMo", "Telecel Cash", "AT Money" or a bank name), their balances, and optionally the last 4 digits of a phone or account number to tell them apart. We never ask for or store full account numbers.
  • Transactions you log: amount, category, account, date and any note you add.
  • Bills, your payday schedule, savings goals and contributions, and your safety buffer.

Where your money records are kept, and why

Your accounts, balances, transactions, bills, goals and safety buffer are kept on your phone first, in Kyeremi's private app storage, with a backup copy on our servers while you're signed in.

  • On your phone: every screen reads from your phone, so Kyeremi works fully without internet or data bundles. Android keeps each app's storage separate, so other apps can't read Kyeremi's data, and phones running modern Android encrypt this storage.
  • Backed up: a few seconds after you make a change (and whenever you're back online), Kyeremi copies it to your backup, so a lost or new phone doesn't mean lost records.

We have turned off Android's automatic app backup for Kyeremi, so your records are not also copied into a Google Drive backup without you knowing. Kyeremi's own backup is the only copy outside your phone.

What the backup holds

  • Backed up: your accounts (names, balances and, if you added them, the last 4 digits), transactions, bills, payday schedule, goals and contributions, and your safety buffer.
  • Never backed up: full account or card numbers (Kyeremi never has them), your MoMo PIN or passwords, and phone-only settings such as reminder times and App lock.
  • Who can read it: through the app, only you. Our database lets each person read and change only their own records. We can access it only to run and support Kyeremi, and we never sell it. Data is encrypted in transit and stored with our database provider, Supabase, whose servers may be outside Ghana.
  • How long it's kept: until you delete your account, which deletes the backup with it. Records you delete in the app are marked deleted in the backup too, so they disappear from your other phones.

New or lost phone? Install Kyeremi and sign in with the same account; your records are restored automatically. Changes made while offline are backed up when you reconnect. If you sign out before they've been backed up, Kyeremi warns you first.

App lock

You can turn on App lock in Settings → Security. Kyeremi then asks for your phone's own fingerprint, face unlock or passcode every time it opens, and again if you've been away for more than a minute. Kyeremi never sees or stores your fingerprint, face or passcode: your phone checks them and only tells Kyeremi whether the check passed.

Settings that stay on your phone

Your reminder time, App lock setting and similar preferences are kept only on your phone. Daily check-in reminders are scheduled on your phone and need no server.

On this website

If you join the waitlist, we store the email address or phone number you give us, the date, the part of the page you signed up from, which phone you use and how you heard about Kyeremi if you choose to tell us, the tag on the link you arrived from (for example a link we shared on WhatsApp), and whether you offered to test Kyeremi before launch. We use these only to decide where to tell people about Kyeremi and when to build the iPhone version. The website has no advertising or tracking cookies.

What we don't collect

  • Your MoMo PIN, bank passwords or card numbers.
  • Your location or contacts.
  • Your text messages. Optional SMS capture (below) reads money messages on your phone; the messages themselves are never sent to us.

Automatic SMS capture (Android, optional)

On Android you can let Kyeremi log your MoMo and bank transactions from their text messages, in Settings → Auto-capture. It is off by default, and Kyeremi explains it before Android asks for the "receive SMS" permission.

  • New messages only. Kyeremi receives SMS as they arrive. It does not open your inbox or read older messages.
  • Only money senders. Kyeremi reads messages from MTN MoMo ("MobileMoney"), Telecel Cash ("T-CASH") and Fidelity Bank. Every other message is ignored as it arrives, without being read or kept.
  • Read on your phone. The amount, the other party, any fee and the date are worked out on your phone, even offline. A money message waits in Kyeremi's private storage on your phone only until it has been read, then it is removed. The text of your messages is never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • You confirm each one. Each message becomes a transaction waiting in Needs Review. Like transactions you type in, it is backed up while you're signed in (see "What the backup holds").
  • Remembered on your phone: which category you chose for a person or shop, so their next message starts there, and which messages were already added, so none is logged twice. These stay on your phone.
  • Turn it off any time in Settings → Auto-capture, or remove the SMS permission in your phone's settings.

You can also share or paste a single money SMS into Kyeremi yourself, on any phone. It is read the same way, on your phone.

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show your Safe-to-Spend, bills, goals and insights. These are worked out from what you enter.
  • To reply when you contact us. If you use "Report a problem", your email includes your app version and phone model so we can help.
  • To email or text you once when Kyeremi launches, if you joined the waitlist. If you also offered to test Kyeremi, we contact you a few times about testing: the invitation, and to ask what you think.

We do not sell your information, show you ads, or use it for anyone else's marketing.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, only to run Kyeremi:

  • Supabase: our account and database provider (sign-in, your profile, your backup and the waitlist).
  • Google: if you choose "Continue with Google", to confirm who you are.
  • Resend: to send sign-in codes by email, if you choose "Continue with email". Resend receives your email address and the code.
  • Vynfy: our SMS provider in Ghana, to send sign-in codes by text message when phone sign-in is available, and the launch text if you joined the waitlist with your phone number. Vynfy receives your phone number and the message.

Some of these providers store data on servers outside Ghana. We choose providers that protect data with encryption and access controls. We may also disclose information if Ghanaian law requires it.

How we protect it

  • Your money records stay on your phone, in storage other apps can't read.
  • Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit.
  • Our database only lets each person read and change their own records.
  • App lock uses your phone's fingerprint, face unlock or passcode (see above).
  • We collect as little as possible: no full account numbers, no PINs, no location.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information and backup until you delete your account. Records on your phone stay there until you sign out, delete your account or uninstall the app. Waitlist emails and phone numbers are kept until Kyeremi launches and we have contacted you, or until you ask us to remove yours.

Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, you can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted, and you can object to how we use it.

  • Delete everything: in the app, go to Profile → Settings → Delete account and data. Can't use the app? See Delete my account.
  • Anything else: email our support email. We reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to Ghana's Data Protection Commission, the regulator for personal data in Ghana.

Children

Kyeremi is for people aged 18 and over. When you create an account, you confirm that you are 18 or older, and the app is listed for adults in app stores. Because Kyeremi never holds money, we don't ask for ID documents to check age. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect.
