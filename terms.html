5. Accuracy: what Verge does, and what you do

Safe-to-Spend is calculated exactly from what you enter: your balances, bills, payday, goals and the expenses you log. When that information is accurate and up to date, the figure is accurate. Verge is built not to overstate it:

  • Income is only counted once it has arrived and is in a balance you entered. Money you're expecting is never counted in advance.
  • Bills due before payday, money set aside for goals and your safety buffer are taken out before you see the figure.

Verge can't see your real accounts, so it can't know about anything you don't enter. Please:

  • enter balances as they really are, checked in your MoMo menu (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash or AT Money) or banking app, and don't round them up;
  • log spending, especially cash, the same day;
  • correct a balance when it no longer matches your real one.

Check your real balances before important spending decisions. How we handle your information is explained in our Privacy Policy.
